# Vocabulary Bank

> Manage your product's word list so everyone uses the same terms.

## Skills

| Command | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| `/synthesizing-vocabulary` | Extract vocabulary from your existing product copy and organize it into tiers — permanent terms, earned terms, and reserved terms |
| `/auditing-vocabulary` | Audit content against your vocabulary bank. Flags off-brand language, terms used in the wrong context, and inconsistencies across channels |

## When to use

- Your product copy is inconsistent across Discord, docs, UI, and social — different words for the same thing
- You're starting a new product and need to define the core terminology before writing begins
- You want to audit existing content to find where the language drifts from your established terms
